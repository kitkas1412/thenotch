# Island and Notch tool

Detailed reference split out of [CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md): the Notch tool, the island window, hover and click policy, modules, file drops, contributions and rendering.

## Notch tool (`Tools/Notch/NotchTool`)

Owns the `IslandController`. `start()` starts it, registers the shortcut and re-registers it when the user changes it (`observeShortcuts`); `stop()` unregisters it and calls `IslandController.stop()`, which closes the island, removes every monitor and observer, cancels pending work and calls `suspend()` on the modules, so Notch off leaves nothing running. `IslandController.start()`/`stop()` are guarded and restartable (a new start begins afresh). `IslandModule.suspend()` defaults to `stop()`; `ClaudeCodeModule.suspend()` stops listening but keeps the hooks, which `stop()` (AI Agents turned off) removes. `ShelfModule` sweeps orphaned dropped files once per process, not on each Notch off/on.

## Intended flow (`thenotch/Core/`)

- `Window/IslandController` — created by `NotchTool`; owns the panel, positions it over the notch, reacts to screen changes.
- `Window/IslandPanel` — borderless, non-activating `NSPanel` sitting at/above the menu bar level, hosting SwiftUI via `NSHostingView`.
- `Geometry/NotchGeometry` — computes the notch rect from `NSScreen` (`safeAreaInsets`, `auxiliaryTopLeftArea` / `auxiliaryTopRightArea`); on screens without a notch it returns a simulated 190pt-wide notch under the menu bar. `targetScreen()` prefers the notched display.
- `State/IslandState` — observable model (collapsed/expanded, content) shared between controller and views.
- `State/HoverPolicy` — pure decisions from the pointer position, with hysteresis (exit region larger than entry).
  - Hovering never opens the island: `hoverAction` sets `IslandState.isHovering` (only while some module has expanded content), which grows the island by `IslandStyle.hoverScale` (`scaleEffect` anchored at the top; under Reduce Motion a `Size.hoverBorder` outline instead) and makes the panel accept mouse events; it ends outside `hoverExitRect` (the grown size).
  - A click opens it: a local `leftMouseDown` monitor on the panel feeds `clickAction` (open/close/none); the open island closes on a click outside (global monitor), a click on the notch, or Esc (global `keyDown` monitor, needs Accessibility, started on open and when Accessibility changes), not when the pointer leaves.
  - The controller feeds `hoverAction` from a global `mouseMoved` monitor plus an `.activeAlways` tracking area on the hosting view (AppKit only sends `mouseMoved` to the key window, and the panel never becomes key); hover is re-evaluated when module settings or the expandable content change (`observeExpandableContent`) and once after the island closes.
  - Only a file drag opens and closes by position (`dragAction`; `closeIfDragLeft()` after it).
- `Modules/IslandModule` + `State/ActivityCenter` — features are `IslandModule`s.
  - To add one, add a case to `ModuleKind` (`thenotch/Settings/AppSettings.swift`); its `rawValue` must equal the module's `id`. `IslandController.applyModuleSettings()` starts/stops modules as they're toggled in Settings.
  - A module publishes a `LiveActivity` (priority, optional expiry, `presents` to open the island by itself) to `state.activities`; `ActivityCenter` picks the one shown in compact mode, and `IslandView` renders that module's `compactLeading`/`compactTrailing` wings beside the notch and its `expandedView` when open.
  - A module reports `hasExpandedContent` (hovering grows the island, and a click opens it, only if some module has content; `IslandState.expandedModule` is `nil` otherwise) and its `compactContentWidth` (wings are that wide plus equal padding, `IslandState.compactPadding`).
  - When two or more modules have content, `IslandView` shows a tab per module left of the notch (`IslandState.showsTabs`, `select(_:)` re-pins until the island closes).
  - Each module also reports its `expandedContentHeight` (content only, with its bottom margin; `IslandState.expandedSize` adds the notch height, the margin below it and, when the tabs don't fit beside the notch, the tab row; the total must stay ≤ `IslandController.panelSize.height`) and may report a narrower `expandedWidth` (default `IslandState.expandedWidth`).
  - `islandDidCollapse()` runs for every module when the island closes.
  - While a file drag has the open island shrinking under the pointer (a narrower tab), `HoverPolicy.ShrinkGrace` keeps the old region open until the pointer is back over it (only the drag path uses it).
- **File drops**: a module conforming to `FileDropReceiving` (the Shelf) takes files dropped on the island.
  - The controller watches global `leftMouseDown/Dragged/Up` events; near the notch it checks the drag pasteboard (`FileDrag`: `changeCount` must have changed since mouse-down, since the pasteboard keeps the previous drag's contents) and opens the island pinned to that module, using the wider `HoverPolicy.dragEntryRect`.
  - `IslandView`'s `onDrop` loads them via `FileDrop` as `DroppedFile`s: file URLs are referenced, other content (file promises, images…) is saved into `Application Support/<bundle id>/Dropped/<uuid>/` and marked `isOwned` — the Shelf deletes those folders when the item leaves, and orphans at start.
  - The shelf persists as `Shelf.json` (bookmarks) via `ShelfPersistence`, read in the background at start (`ShelfStore.load()`; nothing is saved before it finishes) and written on a background queue 1 s after the last change (`ShelfStore.saveNow()` flushes on quit and when the module stops); Quick Look thumbnails are cached in `ShelfThumbnails` (keyed by size too).
  - The shelf opens as a square stack (`ShelfStackView`, 256 pt wide: newest files fanned out, ✕ Clear and … More (an `NSMenu`) in a row above it, "Show All" centered below, or a small AirDrop drop zone when empty; "Drop here" while files are dragged in) and widens to the list (`ShelfListView`) through `ShelfPresentation.showsAll` until the island closes; expiry (`AppSettings.shelfLifetime`) uses one sleeping task, not polling.
  - While files are dragged in, `IslandView` draws the island's border (thick and white once they're over it and not over a zone, `\.isIslandDropTargeted`) and a drop anywhere outside a zone goes to the shown module; modules read `\.isDraggingFiles` from the environment to show their own drop zones (`IslandDropZone`s, which only report their frames: `IslandView` has the single `onDrop`, outside the clip shape, and routes files to the zone under the pointer; an `onDrop` inside the clipped island gets a misplaced AppKit view and never receives drops).
  - Dragging out: shelf tiles (and the stack, which drags every file) start an AppKit dragging session (`ShelfDrag`, so Finder can *move* the file and we learn the operation; SwiftUI's `onDrag` only copies). Any completed drop removes the item from the shelf (`ShelfDrag.Outcome`); after a copy (a browser upload, Mail…) its owned file is kept for the next launch's orphan cleanup, since the app may still be reading it and call `\.beginDragOut`; the island stays open until the mouse button is released (polled). It also stays open while one of our menus tracks (`NSMenu.didBeginTracking`).
- **Island contributions** (`Core/Modules/IslandContributions`): a tool (not a `ModuleKind`) can add a module to the island.
  - `AppDelegate` owns one `IslandContributions` (`ToolContext.islandContributions`); the tool `add`s/`remove`s a `ContributedIslandModule` (an `IslandModule` with `attach(activities:)`/`detach()`), which the controller attaches after the built-in modules (`state.modules`) while the Notch tool runs and detaches when the island stops or the module is removed; the island never calls `start()`/`stop()`/`suspend()` on it, the tool does.
  - `IslandController` sets `contributions.host` (`IslandHosting`) while it runs: `isHostAvailable` is observable, `show(id)` → `toggleContribution(id:)` opens the island on the module, selects it if another is shown, or closes it if it is shown (`ContributionToggle.decide`; false while Notch is off, hidden or blocked), `close()` closes the island.
  - A contributed module is shown only when pinned (shortcut, `show`, tab): `IslandState.expandedModule` doesn't pick it through its own activity, and `PresentationPolicy` never switches an island that shows a keyboard module to a banner or call.
  - `IslandModule` also has `tabTitle`/`tabSymbol` (default from `ModuleKind`), `takesKeyboard`, `excludesFromCapture` and `handleKeyDown(_:)` (all off by default).
  - While the shown module `takesKeyboard`, the controller makes `IslandPanel` key without activating the app (`acceptsKeyboard`, `canBecomeKey`; the frontmost app is remembered and re-activated if needed on release) and `IslandPanel.sendEvent` offers `keyDown` to `handleKeyDown` (not while an input method has marked text); an unhandled Esc closes the island, and the island closes when the panel stops being key (⌘Tab, a click on another app).
  - While the shown module `excludesFromCapture`, the panel's `sharingType` is `.none` for the whole opening (switching tabs never restores it) and goes back to `.readOnly` 0.8 s after the island closes (`ShownModulePolicy`, `IslandController.captureRestoreDelay`); the controller applies both from `updateShownModulePolicies()`.
  - These decisions are pure (`ContributionToggle`, `ShownModulePolicy`, `PresentationPolicy`).
- `State/AmbientConditions` — display asleep, screen locked, session inactive, Low Power Mode; the controller keeps `state.ambient` current and views read `\.allowsAmbientAnimation` to stop ambient animation (the level meter).
- `UI/IslandView` + `UI/NotchShape` — SwiftUI rendering of the island and its notch-matching shape.
  - The island is always black: it renders under a forced dark color scheme, so views use semantic styles (`.primary`, `.secondary`) rather than `.white.opacity(…)`.
  - The design system is documented in `DESIGN.md`: tokens in `Core/UI/DesignSystem/` (`IslandStyle` spacing/radius/size/symbol sizes/motion, `IslandColors` with `IslandFill` levels that strengthen under Increase Contrast and `IslandSignal` status colors, `IslandTypography` `Font.island*` styles) and shared views in `Core/UI/Components/` (`.islandIcon`/`.islandText` button styles, `IslandEmptyState`, `IslandDropZone`, `IslandProgressBar`, `.islandContentMargins()`). Views use these tokens, not literal sizes, opacities or colors.
